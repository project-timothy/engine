## Ask Tim

- Who you are with Tim: your name, what you do, whose spending you look over and who looks over yours. And how to do anything with Tim, in plain answers. *"Who am I in Tim, and how do I send a receipt?"*
- What is coming due, and how many days are left. *"What's coming due this month?"*
- What is waiting for someone's yes or no. *"Is anything waiting on me?"*
- Which bills are still unpaid, the soonest first, and the total. *"What bills do we still owe?"*
- Find a bill by who sent it or its number. *"Find the bill from the water company."*
- Whether this morning's work ran, and how it went. *"Did this morning's work run?"*
- Where the month's closing stands. *"Where does closing September stand?"*
- Your expense reports, their totals, and whether you have been paid back. *"Have I been paid back for August?"*
- A month of receipts in dollars. A receipt paid in another currency shows what you paid and its dollar value on that day. *"Show me my October receipts."*
- Where your year stands: each month's receipts and total, what's still waiting, and totals by category. *"Where's my year?"*
- Your own Claude reads the receipts you sent in a pile: the day, the amount, and who you paid. *"Read my receipts."*
- Before you buy something, what your ministry's own rules say about it, with the handbook and page. *"Can I buy a $1,400 projector for the school?"*

## Tell Tim

- Say yes or no to something waiting on you. Under your ministry's limit, just say so; over it, Tim asks you to confirm with Face ID. *"Approve Ruth's September report."*
- Add a receipt: say what you paid, then snap the photo from the link Tim sends. *"Here's a receipt, 4,300 rupees for the bus to Pokhara."*
- Put a receipt under a category, like Travel or Meals. *"That bus ticket goes under Travel."*
- Say how much a receipt was, after you've snapped it. *"That last receipt was 1,250 rupees."*
- Say what a drive was for. Tim keeps the miles and times your phone tracked, never where you went. *"Tuesday's drive was the church visit in Pokhara."*
- Catching up? One link takes a whole pile at once (receipt photos, PDFs, your bank's statements, a school list) and works for a day. *"I've got a year of receipts to catch up on."*
- Say which day a receipt is from, so it lands in its own month at that day's rate. *"That one's from March 5."*
- What a receipt reads is kept as a reading. The amount counts once you agree to it. *"Read the next receipt and tell me what it says."*
- One yes for a month of receipts, once Tim has walked you through what it read. *"Yes, September's right."*
- Send a month to your reviewer for their yes, all of it at once, when it's ready. *"Send September for review."*
- Send the statements your bank's website exports: the ministry's account counts in full, and from your own account Tim keeps only the lines you pick. *"Here are my bank statements."*
- Pick which lines of your own account were the ministry's. The rest never stay with Tim. *"The taxi and the supermarket were ministry; the rest is mine."*
- Send your list of scholarships and school fees, and each one counts in its own month with no receipt needed. *"Here's my scholarship list."*
- Say what a bank line was for when you have no receipt for it. *"The guest house was the team's hotel in Kathmandu."*

## What Tim does on its own

- Reads the bills that come in, files them, and asks a person before anything is paid.
- Notices money on its way in and checks it off when the bank shows it arrived.
- Sends each person one short page a week that leads with what needs them.
- Gets the month ready to close and seals it once a person says yes.
- Remembers the dates that matter, like filings, renewals, passports and visas, and reminds you weeks ahead.
- Turns receipts into an expense report and follows it until the money is paid back.
- Collects documents from the ministry's mailbox for the other lanes.
- Turns the ministry's handbook into rules, and asks a person to say yes to each one before it counts.
- Notices when the list of projects or funds drifts out of step with the books.
- Sends each decision to the right person, emails them when it waits (at most once a day), tells a reviewer when a month is ready, and covers for someone who is away.
- Files the hours people report.

## What Tim asks when you start

1. What kind of work will Tim keep the books for?
2. What is the legal name, the one on your tax filings?
3. And what's your name and email? Like: Pat Lee, pat@example.org
4. What do you do there?
5. What time zone are you in?
6. What month does your financial year start?
7. How is the business set up for taxes?
8. Is it a church, or another kind of charity?
9. How does the work get paid for?
10. Does anyone look over your spending, like a sponsor, a board member or an accountant? Their name and email, like: Dale Ortiz, dale@example.org. Or leave it blank.
11. Up to what amount can someone okay their own bill? Above it, a second person does.
12. Above what amount should a bill need two people to okay it?
13. Up to what amount can someone okay a request just by telling their AI? Above it, they confirm on their phone with Face ID.
14. Who else is involved, and what do they do? One per line, like: Sam Lee = board
15. Does anyone look after other people's spending, like a regional director over the missionaries in her region? One per line, like: Ann Lee = Sam Ortiz, Mei Tan
16. Any words Tim should never use in what it writes for you, like 'missionary' for work in a sensitive country? Leave it blank if there are none.
17. American or British spelling?
